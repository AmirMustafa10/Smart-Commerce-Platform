# Store Management

## Overview

The `stores` application is responsible for managing tenant-related information.

Current functionality includes:

- Updating store information
- Activating stores
- Deactivating stores

## Store Settings

Store owners can update store information through a dedicated settings page.

The settings interface allows direct modification of store details while preserving tenant ownership.

## Store Activation

Instead of permanently deleting stores, the system supports activation and deactivation.

This approach preserves business data, historical records, and relationships with products, staff members, and future orders.

## Authorization

Store management operations are restricted to store owners.

Staff members cannot modify store settings or activation status.
